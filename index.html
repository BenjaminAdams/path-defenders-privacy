Privacy Policy – Path Defenders

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Path Defenders ("we", "our", or "the game") is a mobile tower defense game developed by Benjamin Adams. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the game and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We do not create user accounts or directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

The following data may be collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the game:

  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Purchase data: If you make in-app purchases, Google Play processes payment information. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Crash data: Basic crash and performance data may be collected by Google Play to help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To display advertisements within the game
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To improve game stability and performance

3. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – Advertising
  • Google Play Billing – In-app purchases

4. Children's Privacy

Path Defenders is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data collected by third-party services is subject to their respective retention policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. For data held by Google, please refer to your Google account settings at myaccount.google.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
