Path Defenders – Delete Your Data

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Path Defenders lets you optionally sign in with Google, Facebook, Apple, or an email address to back up your game progress across devices. If you would like to delete your account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

What data we store

  • Your Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Your email address and display name (if provided by your sign-in provider)
  • Your game save data (units, gold, progress)

We do not sell your data to third parties.

How to delete your data

Option 1 – Delete from inside the game

  1. Open Path Defenders
  2. Tap the Settings icon
  3. Go to the Saves tab
  4. Tap Sign Out under Cloud Save

This disconnects cloud sync. To permanently delete all stored data, also send us a deletion request by email (see Option 2).

Option 2 – Email us a deletion request

  1. Send an email to benjiballin@hotmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request – Path Defenders
  3. Include the email address associated with your game account

We will permanently delete your account and all associated save data within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase authentication account (UID, email, display name)
  • All cloud save slots linked to your account

Note: local saves stored only on your device are not affected. To remove those, uninstall the app.

Contact

For any questions about your data, contact: benjiballin@hotmail.com
